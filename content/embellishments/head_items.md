Bones, cod, end rods, feathers, fence gates, leads, lightning rods and spyglasses render in a special way on the head.

## How to wear them

Put the item into the helmet slot of your inventory.

Banners can also be put on the head of an entity with a **dispenser**, as long as its head slot is empty.
