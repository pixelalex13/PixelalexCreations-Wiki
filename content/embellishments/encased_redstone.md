# :icon[images/encased_redstone.png]{size=36} Encased Redstone

Encased Redstone works like redstone dust, but as a full block.

## How it works

- It picks up a signal from all six sides, including from above and below.
- Like redstone dust, the signal gets weaker by one level for every block in a line.
- It powers redstone components and solid blocks on all six sides.
- When powered, it shows redstone particles.

::: info
The block that receives the signal from Encased Redstone gets one level less than the Encased Redstone itself. A lever next to one Encased Redstone block powers a lamp on the other side with strength 13.
:::
