Embellishments adds a number of small additions that fit the vibe of the vanilla game.

:loader[Fabric, Quilt]

## Features

- **[Eye Sensor](eye_sensor):** a redstone block that reacts to players looking at it.
- **[Encased Redstone](encased_redstone):** redstone dust as a full block.
- **[Defusing TNT](defusing_tnt):** defuse TNT with shears and rearm it with gunpowder.
- **[Head Items](head_items):** wear banners, glass and other items on your head.
- **[Invisible Name Tag](invisible_name_tag):** hide your name tag and disappear from the locator bar.
