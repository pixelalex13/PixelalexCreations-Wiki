## Defusing TNT

Hold **shears** in your main hand and use them on a TNT block.

- The TNT turns into **Defused TNT** and cannot explode anymore.
- One gunpowder drops on top of the block.
- The shears lose one point of durability.

## Rearming TNT

Hold **gunpowder** in your main hand and use it on Defused TNT.

- The block turns back into normal TNT.
- One gunpowder is used up (not in Creative mode).
