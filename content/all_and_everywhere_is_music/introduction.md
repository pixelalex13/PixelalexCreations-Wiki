All and Everywhere is Music lets every music track play everywhere. The main menu, every biome, the Nether, the End, underwater and even the credits all share one big playlist with all music of the game.

::: info Remake in progress
The pack is currently being remade. Some details on this wiki may change with the next update.
:::
