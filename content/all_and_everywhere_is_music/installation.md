## Steps

1. Download the version for your Minecraft version from [Pixelalex Creations](https://www.pixelalexcreations.net/resource_packs/#all_and_everywhere_is_music). See the [version list](introduction#supported-versions).
2. Open Minecraft and go to **Options › Resource Packs › Open Pack Folder**.
3. Put the downloaded `.zip` file into this folder. Do not unzip it.
4. Back in the game, move the pack from the left to the right side and click **Done**.

::: info Client-side only
The pack only changes your game. It works on any server and in singleplayer.
:::

::: tip
Make sure the **Music** volume under **Options › Music & Sounds** is not set to 0.
:::
