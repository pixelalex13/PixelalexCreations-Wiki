You start on a small island floating in the void. Everything around you is empty, so every block counts. Do not fall off: below you there is only the void.

## Goal

There is no fixed goal. Expand your island, build farms and work your way through the [advancements](advancements). The final challenge is to defeat the Ender Dragon and claim the Dragon Egg.

## Getting started

The advancements follow the natural progression of Skyblock. A rough path:

1. **Chop down your tree** and plant the saplings you get from it.
2. **Build a cobblestone generator** with your ice and lava, and craft stone tools.
3. **Reach the sand island** and loot the chest there. Break the cactus without letting the sand fall.
4. **Grow food:** melons, pumpkins, sugar cane and wheat from bonemealed grass.
5. **Build a Nether portal** and ignite it by burning wood with your lava.
6. In the Nether, collect **mushrooms and glowstone** from the Nether island.
7. **Build a mob grinder**, get iron and armor.
8. **Cure zombie villagers** to start your own village and trade with them.
9. Find a **Nether Fortress** and use its boundaries to spawn blazes and wither skeletons.
10. **Craft Eyes of Ender**, find the End Portal frame and defeat the Ender Dragon.

## The void

The whole map is void, but biomes and structure boundaries still exist. That means:

- Mobs that only spawn in certain biomes or structures still spawn there, as long as there are blocks to spawn on.
- Nether Fortresses, Strongholds and other structures can be found by their boundaries, even though nothing is built.
- The End Portal frame exists, so you can reach the End and beat the game.
