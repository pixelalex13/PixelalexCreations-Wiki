Every block you walk costs you health. WalkingEqualsDamage is a small challenge mod that turns every step into a risk.

:loader[Fabric, Quilt]

## How it works

- You take damage every time you move into a new block horizontally.
- By default, each block costs you **half a heart**. You can change this in the [configuration](configuration).
- Only horizontal movement counts. Moving straight up or down (jumping in place, pillaring, climbing a ladder, falling straight down) is free.
- It does not matter how you move: walking, sprinting, sneaking, swimming, riding, flying with an elytra or being pushed all count.
- Players in Creative or Spectator mode take no damage.

::: tip
Plan your routes. Every block counts, so the shortest path is the safest one.
:::
