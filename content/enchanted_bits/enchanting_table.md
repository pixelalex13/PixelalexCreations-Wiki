## Chiseled Bookshelves

A Chiseled Bookshelf with **three or more** filled slots now powers an Enchanting Table, just like a normal bookshelf.

- Any item in a slot counts: books, enchanted books, written books and Shifting Books.
- A Chiseled Bookshelf with 3 to 6 books gives the same power as one normal bookshelf.
- The usual rules still apply: the shelf has to be placed like a normal bookshelf around the table.

::: info Why three books?
The minimum prevents the Chiseled Bookshelf from becoming a complete alternative to the normal bookshelf.
:::

## Blocks between table and bookshelves

In vanilla, the space between an Enchanting Table and its bookshelves has to be empty. With Enchanted Bits, you can place most blocks that are not a full cube there, such as torches, carpets, slabs, stairs, fences, candles or flowers. The bookshelves still power the table.

The full list of allowed blocks is controlled by a tag. See [Tags](tags).
