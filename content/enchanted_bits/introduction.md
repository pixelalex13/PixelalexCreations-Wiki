Enchanted Bits improves several small aspects of the enchanting system. It does not overhaul anything, it just makes enchanting slightly better.

:loader[Fabric, Quilt]

## Features

- **[Shifting Book](shifting_book):** a new item that turns 5 levels into a random enchanted book.
- **[Enchanting Table](enchanting_table):** Chiseled Bookshelves can power an Enchanting Table, and more blocks can be placed between the table and its bookshelves.
- **[Other Changes](other_changes):** anvils no longer have a level cap, and Frost Walker is stronger and works while riding.
