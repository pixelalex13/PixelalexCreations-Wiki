The Petrified Oak Slab is a leftover from old versions of Minecraft: a wooden slab that behaves like stone and cannot burn. Zephyr makes it craftable and adds a Petrified Slab for every other wood type.

## Crafting

Every Petrified Slab is crafted from a Stone Slab and the matching wood slab. You get 2 slabs.
