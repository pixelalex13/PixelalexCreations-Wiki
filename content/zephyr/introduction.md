Zephyr is a vanilla-plus mod that restores removed and unused content, fixes overlooked bugs, and refines underutilized mechanics. Everything is designed to feel like it could be part of the vanilla game.

:loader[Fabric, Quilt]

## Features

**Removed and Unused Content**
- **[Clovers & Potions](clovers_and_potions):** a new plant that lets you brew Luck and Bad Luck, plus the Potion of Decay from Wither Roses.
- **[Illusioner](illusioner):** joins raids again and drops the new Vitalis Bottle.
- **[Paintings](paintings):** the unused Earth, Fire, Water and Wind paintings are now obtainable.
- **[Petrified Slabs](petrified_slabs):** for every wood type.
- **[Formatting Codes](formatting_codes):** use the `§` character to format text in survival.
- **[Killer Bunny](killer_bunny):** a rare variant of every newly spawned rabbit.

**Underutilized Mechanics**
- **[Bone Meal](bone_meal)** works on more plants.
- **[Item Frames](item_frames)** and **[Armor Stands](armor_stands)** can be made invisible, and there are three new armor stand variants.
- **[Void Banner](void_banner):** a reworked End City banner.
- **[Other Tweaks](other_tweaks):** enchantment levels up to 255, Wind Burst in Ominous Vaults, HD banners on shields and more spawn eggs.

**Overlooked Bugs**
- **[Bug Fixes](bug_fixes)** for small issues in Pillager Outposts and Trial Chambers.
