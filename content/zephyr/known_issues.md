These issues are known and will be fixed in a future update.

::: details Pumpkins and Melons grow on any block with Bone Meal
When you use Bone Meal on a fully grown stem, the Pumpkin or Melon can grow on any full block, for example Stone or Glass. In vanilla, they only grow on dirt-like blocks.
:::

::: details Cactus grown with Bone Meal can break
Cactus grown with Bone Meal does not check its surroundings. If a block is next to a new cactus segment, the cactus breaks at the next block update.
:::

::: details Double Petrified Slabs only drop one slab
When you break two Petrified Slabs that were placed together as one full block, only one slab drops instead of two.
:::
