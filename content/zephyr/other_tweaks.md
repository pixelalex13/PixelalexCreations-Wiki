## Enchantment levels

Enchantment levels are now shown as Roman numerals up to the maximum level of **255**. In vanilla, levels above X are shown as a raw text key.

## Wind Burst in Ominous Vaults

Ominous Vaults can now give Wind Burst books from **level I to III**, not only level I.

## Shields with banners

Banners on shields now use the same HD texture as placed banners, instead of a shrunk-down version.
