Four paintings have been in the game for a long time but could never be placed in survival. With Zephyr, they can now appear when you place a normal painting:

| Painting | Size |
|---|:---:|
| Earth | 2×2 |
| Fire | 2×2 |
| Water | 2×2 |
| Wind | 2×2 |

Like every other painting, the result is random. Place a painting on a wall with enough space for a 2×2 painting to get a chance at one of them.
