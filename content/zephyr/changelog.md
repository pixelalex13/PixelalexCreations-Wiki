## v1.2
::: changelog
## Additions

**Removed and Unused Content**
- The Illusioner now has a chance to appear in later raid waves
- The Illusioner now drops emeralds and a new type of bottle, the Vitalis Bottle. It is nearly identical to the Ominous Bottle, but instead grants one of 5 levels of Health Boost for 50 minutes. Along with this, a new data component has been added that determines the level of the Vitalis Bottle, ranging from 1 to 5
[gray]*Because of this, the Health Boost effect has been added to the advancement "How Did We Get Here?".*[/]
- Health Boost has also been given a custom particle texture, similar to Raid Omen or Trial Omen
- The Illusioner now has its own spawn egg in the creative inventory, and the existing Wither and Ender Dragon spawn eggs are now available there as well
[gray]*The spawn egg feature has been ported over from another mod by pixelalex13, Embellishments. This means that, from this version on, this mod is incompatible with any Embellishments version below 1.3.1.*[/]
- The Killer Bunny now has a 1% chance of replacing any newly spawned rabbit
- Added the Petrified Poplar Slab with a respective recipe using a poplar slab and a stone slab (only in Minecraft 26.3)

**Underutilized Mechanics**
- Added 3 more variants of the armor stand: "Armor Stand with Arms", "Small Armor Stand", and "Small Armor Stand with Arms". These are crafted slightly differently than the normal armor stand and are exactly what their names state
- Added the ability to make all armor stand variants invisible by shift-clicking them with an empty hand
[gray]*Similar to Barriers or Item Frames in this mod, holding the respective armor stand variant makes all nearby invisible armor stands of that variant visible.*[/]
:::

## v1.0
::: changelog
## Additions

- Added English and German language support

**Removed and Unused Content**
- Unused paintings like Earth, Fire, Water and Wind are now obtainable in survival
- Added Petrified Slabs for every Wood Type with a respective recipe using the specific wood slab and a stone slab
- Added a new grass-like plant "Clovers", that spawns in patches similar to bushes and has a small chance to drop either a Clover or a Rotten Clover which can be used to brew Luck and Bad Luck Potions
- The wither rose can be used to brew the Potion of Decay
[gray]*Because of this the Luck, Bad Luck and Wither Effect have been added to the advancement "A Furious Cocktail" and the Luck and Bad Luck Effect also to "How Did We Get Here?".*[/]

**Overlooked Bugs**
- Fixed the Ominous Banner on Outposts, whose patterns were visible in the tooltip despite not being intended to show
- Placed the missing ladder in the trial chamber atrium_1
- Placed the 2 missing tuff blocks under the fountain in atrium_1

**Underutilized Mechanics**
- Enchantment level symbols now go up to the limit (255)
- The Wind Burst enchantment can now appear from levels 1-3 in an ominous vault
- The textures of shields with banners are now no longer shrunk down
- Replaced the old end city banner with a new reworked "Void Banner" that is also available in the Creative Menu and more in line with the Ominous Banner
- Bone meal can now be used on sugar cane, cactus, nether warts, small flower and pumpkin and melon stems to instantly grow pumpkins or melons
