Bone Meal now works on more plants:

| Plant | Effect |
|---|---|
| :icon[images/items/sugar_cane.png]{size=20} Sugar Cane | Instantly grows to its full height of 3 blocks |
| :icon[images/items/cactus.png]{size=20} Cactus | Instantly grows to its full height of 3 blocks |
| :icon[images/items/nether_wart.png]{size=20} Nether Wart | Grows 1 to 3 stages |
| :icon[images/items/poppy.png]{size=20} Small Flowers | Spreads a copy of the flower to a free spot nearby |
| :icon[images/items/pumpkin_seeds.png]{size=20} Pumpkin and Melon Stems | Grow 2 to 5 stages. A fully grown stem instantly grows a Pumpkin or Melon next to it |

::: info
Wither Roses, Torchflowers and Golden Dandelions cannot be spread with Bone Meal.
:::

A Pumpkin or Melon can only grow if there is a free spot next to the stem, and the stem does not have a fruit attached yet.
