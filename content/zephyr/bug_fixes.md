Zephyr fixes a few small bugs in vanilla structures.

## Pillager Outposts

The Ominous Banner on Pillager Outposts showed its patterns in the tooltip, although it is not supposed to. This is now hidden, like on every other Ominous Banner.

## Trial Chambers

In the atrium variant `atrium_1`:

- The missing ladder has been added.
- The two missing Tuff blocks under the fountain have been added.
