Step into a world where spirits remember what time has forgotten. Spirit Scape adds a new material, new equipment, a whole system for restoring lost items and new structures to explore.

:loader[Fabric, Quilt]

::: info Beta
Spirit Scape is still in **Beta**. Features may change, and some items exist only until a proper way to obtain them is added.
:::

## Features

- **[Spirit Crystal](spirit_crystal):** a rare new ore found deep underground, also usable as an armor trim material.
- **[Tools & Weapons](tools_and_weapons)** and **[Armor](armor):** a full Spirit Crystal set, from sword to Nautilus Armor.
- **[Muddy Items](muddy_items):** dirty tools, armor and bundles hidden all over the world. Clean them in the **[Cleaning Station](cleaning_station)** with **[Soap Bars](soap_bars)** to find out what they really are.
- **[Graveyards](graveyard):** a rare new structure in six biome variants, with graves, a crypt full of **[Urns](urn)** and the **[Gravedigger](gravedigger)** villager.
- **[Dimensional Rift](dimensional_rift):** a rare world event that brings the mysterious Spirit Manifest into the world.

::: info
While Spirit Scape is in Beta, only the newest Minecraft version is supported. Older versions can still be downloaded, but they do not get updates or fixes. 1.21.11 only has Alpha v1.1, without Muddy Items, Graveyards and everything else added in the Beta.
:::
