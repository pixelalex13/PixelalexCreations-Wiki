Muddy Items are dirty tools, armor and bundles that can be found all over the world. What is hidden under the mud only becomes clear once you clean them in the [Cleaning Station](cleaning_station). A Muddy Axe could turn out to be a Wooden Axe, but also a Netherite or even a Spirit Crystal Axe.

## All Muddy Items

There are 13 Muddy Items:

| Muddy Item | Cleans into |
|---|---|
| Muddy Sword | A sword |
| Muddy Spear | A spear |
| Muddy Pickaxe | A pickaxe |
| Muddy Axe | An axe |
| Muddy Shovel | A shovel |
| Muddy Hoe | A hoe |
| Muddy Helmet | A helmet |
| Muddy Chestplate | A chestplate |
| Muddy Leggings | Leggings |
| Muddy Boots | Boots |
| Muddy Horse Armor | Horse armor |
| Muddy Nautilus Armor | Nautilus armor |
| Muddy Bundled Minerals | A bundle filled with minerals |

The quality of the result depends on the [Soap Bar](soap_bars) you use. See [Cleaning Station](cleaning_station#results) for all chances.
