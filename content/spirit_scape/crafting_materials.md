## :icon[images/items/spirit_plasma.png]{size=30} Spirit Plasma

A crafting material dropped by **Vexes**.

- Drop chance: **10%** per Vex. Looting has no effect.
- It can also be sold to the [Gravedigger](gravedigger).

::: info
The way to get Spirit Plasma is planned to change in a later version.
:::

## :icon[images/items/soul.gif]{size=30} Soul

A crafting material dropped by **Soul Sand**, similar to Flint from Gravel, but much rarer.

| Tool | Drop chance |
|---|:---:|
| No Fortune | 0.1% |
| Fortune I | 0.14% |
| Fortune II | 0.25% |
| Fortune III | 1% |

Silk Touch always drops the Soul Sand block itself.

::: info
This is a temporary solution and might change if there is a better way to obtain Souls.
:::
