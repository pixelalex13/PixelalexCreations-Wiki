Soap Bars are used in the [Cleaning Station](cleaning_station) to clean [Muddy Items](muddy_items). There are three tiers. Better soap takes longer, but gives a much higher chance of a rare result.

## Tiers

| Soap Bar | Uses | Cleaning time | Quality |
|---|:---:|:---:|---|
| :icon[images/items/crude_soap_bar.png]{size=20} Crude Soap Bar | 5 | 15 seconds | Mostly basic items |
| :icon[images/items/refined_soap_bar.png]{size=20} Refined Soap Bar | 10 | 30 seconds | Mostly iron and copper tier, small chance of Spirit Crystal |
| :icon[images/items/purified_soap_bar.png]{size=20} Purified Soap Bar | 15 | 45 seconds | Mostly diamond and Netherite tier, 10% chance of Spirit Crystal |

Each cleaning uses up one use of the Soap Bar. Soap Bars do not stack.

The exact chances are listed on the [Cleaning Station](cleaning_station#results) page.

## Other ways to get them

- **Trading:** the [Gravedigger](gravedigger) sells all three tiers.
- **Graveyards:** the Gravedigger's chest in a [Graveyard](graveyard) always contains a Crude or Refined Soap Bar.
