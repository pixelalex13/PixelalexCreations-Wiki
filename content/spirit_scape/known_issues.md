These issues are known and will be fixed in a future Beta.

::: details Dimensional Rift can open in the wrong dimension
The rift picks a random player from all dimensions, but always opens in the Overworld. If that player is in the Nether or the End, the Spirit Manifest drops in the Overworld at the same coordinates, where nobody will see it.
:::

::: details /spawnevent opens the rift near a random player
`/spawnevent dimensional_rift` opens the rift near a random online player, not near the player who ran the command. The success message still says "around you".
:::
