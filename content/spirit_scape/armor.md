The Spirit Crystal armor set includes a **Helmet, Chestplate, Leggings, Boots, Horse Armor and Nautilus Armor**.

It is similar to Netherite, but has **no knockback resistance**. As a trade-off, every piece gives **one more armor point**.

## Stats

| Piece | Spirit Crystal | Netherite |
|---|:---:|:---:|
| Helmet | [green]4[/] | 3 |
| Chestplate | [green]9[/] | 8 |
| Leggings | [green]7[/] | 6 |
| Boots | [green]4[/] | 3 |
| Horse Armor | [green]20[/] | 19 |
| Toughness | 3 | 3 |
| Knockback resistance | [red]0[/] | 0.1 |
| Durability multiplier | 37 | 37 |
| Enchantability | 15 | 15 |

Spirit Crystal armor is repaired with [Spirit Crystals](spirit_crystal) and can be trimmed like any other armor.

## :icon[images/items/spirit_crystal_horse_armor.png]{size=30} :icon[images/items/spirit_crystal_nautilus_armor.png]{size=30} Horse and Nautilus Armor

Spirit Crystal Horse Armor and Nautilus Armor cannot be crafted. They can only be obtained by cleaning a Muddy Horse Armor or Muddy Nautilus Armor in the [Cleaning Station](cleaning_station), see [Muddy Items](muddy_items).

| Item | Crude Soap | Refined Soap | Purified Soap |
|---|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| Spirit Crystal Horse Armor | – | 1% | 5% |
| Spirit Crystal Nautilus Armor | – | 4% | 11% |
